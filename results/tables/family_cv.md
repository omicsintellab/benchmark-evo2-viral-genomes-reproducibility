## Table R1-D. Leave-one-family-out, Evo 2 — pooled vs per-family

The pooled R² is computed over the stacked out-of-fold predictions; the per-family R² is computed **within** each held-out family. A pooled R² well above the per-family values means the probe is tracking between-family differences (i.e. family identity) rather than within-family architecture — the confound both reviewers raised.
